Help: Control edges

Use this page when you need definitions or calculation details. The data tab stays focused on saved results.

What this tab answers

Who can control this object? and What can this principal control? Each row is a saved permission-derived relationship, not a completed operation.

Support

  • Established: captured evidence supports the capability.
  • Not established: saved evidence disproves the supported case.
  • Unknown: required evidence or analysis is unresolved.

Expectedness

Expected means the relationship matches a recognized administrative pattern. It does not mean safe. Unknown expectedness remains separate from unknown permission support.

Evidence, requester, membership

Permission proof, a concrete account's authorization check, and stored group membership answer different questions. Membership alone does not prove requester authorization.

Coverage

Incomplete capture, unsupported analysis, and processing that was not scheduled are shown separately. None of them means no control exists.

Not a Tier 0 path

A control edge is one building block. The separate Tier 0 tab shows whether supported relationships were successfully chained to a qualified Tier 0 target.

How control relationships are counted

One group-held permission remains one permission relationship. Member attribution, requester checks, and later routes are supporting answers and are not added to that permission count. Full-control summaries may group supporting permissions without deleting their original evidence.

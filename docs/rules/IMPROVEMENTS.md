# Rule-Review Improvement Tags

> Cross-cutting improvements identified during per-rule code-first verification are
> captured under `## 12. Defects / deferred items` and `## 13. Improvement suggestions`
> in each `SEC-XXX.md` card. Each non-resolved item is prefixed with a tag from the
> taxonomy below so a future improvement pass can aggregate them by theme.

## How to use

- During a rule audit, capture observations in the appropriate `Â§12` (accepted defect
  / known limitation) or `Â§13` (non-blocking improvement) section of the rule card.
- Prefix the item with one of the tags below in backticks, e.g.
  `` `[IMP-DEL-OBJ]` `` — _description._
- Resolved items keep their historical entry but **do not** carry a tag.
- After all rules are reviewed, run:

  ```powershell
  Select-String -Path docs\rules\SEC-*.md -Pattern "\[IMP-[A-Z-]+\]"
  ```

  to assemble the full improvement backlog grouped by tag. Each tag should become a
  single themed change rather than a per-rule patch.

## Why the lab and not the bench

`next/artifacts/p280-shape-profile/` records what the synthetic bench is: a forest where
every descriptor holds exactly 521 entries, every name is exactly 97 bytes, and there is
not one object-type GUID or one `SELF` entry in it. It was generated by this codebase, so
it can only contain what the code already believed - `F-S44-2`.

The lab is a real Active Directory. It is far too small for size or timing claims and
misleading about reuse, but it has the thing the bench cannot have: **the shapes nobody
thought to generate.** Its profile is at `lab-profile.json`.

## And what the bench could not have told us

| `permission_entry`, by trustee | Rows | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **`S-1-5-10`, `PRINCIPAL SELF`** | **34,593** | **94.8%** |
| Every other trustee in the forest | 1,891 | 5.2% |

**Ninety-five per cent of the table `P-279` built to shrink is one SID.**

`SharedPermissionStorage.ContentKey` serialises the whole `PermissionEntry` including
`Trustee.Name`, and `ObjectPermissions` resolves `S-1-5-10` to `This object (Alice)`
against the object being read. So Alice's `SELF` entry and Bob's hash differently and never
share a row, and every object carrying one gets its own. That is exactly what keeps `P-279`
*correct* - nothing is misattributed - and it is why the table it built barely deduplicates
on a real directory.

**The bench reported none of this**, because it has no `SELF` entries at all: 24,098 rows
against 17,164,866 occurrences, a 712:1 collapse, and a clean success. The lab says the
success is 5% of a real forest's rows.

`P-285` would take `permission_entry` here from 36,484 to roughly 1,900 - the raw `SELF`
ACE shapes collapse to a handful once the resolved name is out of the identity. On the
production capture the same defect accounts for **20,454,613 occurrences**.

## What this baseline is for

- **`P-285`** - re-judge and expect `permission_entry` near 1,900. That is the check.
- **`P-283`** - the binding change should alter no count here at all. A count that moves is
  the finding.
- **`P-280`** - `permission_classification_entry` should fall from 209,252 toward the
  per-descriptor figure. **The lab cannot price the saving**: its descriptors are reused
  22.3 times on average against production's 2.46, so keying per descriptor looks nine
  times better here than it will on the owner's forest. Correctness here, size elsewhere.
